How to share

  • Click the Markette button in the toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+M (change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts).
  • Right-click a page, a selection, a link or an image and pick “Share … with Markette”.
  • Selected text is quoted in the post; the page address becomes the link.
